# The long way around

## Visual direction

A paper-coloured editorial archive with an asymmetrical illustrated opening. The name carries the identity; the illustrated portrait shares its space rather than becoming a profile thumbnail. One orange full stop, quiet numbering and occasional marginal notes give the pages a human hand. There are no biker graphics, dashboards or decorative maps.

The design shifts from large film to smaller paper, from an archival player to an interactive screen sequence. The main page does not give eighteen projects equal billing. The complete index does.

## Type and palette

Arial/Helvetica provides a neutral grotesk voice, Georgia introduces an editorial italic, and Courier New handles occasional production metadata. Local system fonts avoid downloads and late font swaps. Body copy is 16px or more; secondary metadata is intentionally smaller. Warm ivory #eeeae1, charcoal #262620 and burnt orange #c84322 form the base palette. Artwork supplies the remaining colour. No filters are applied to project artwork.

## Hierarchy and film selection

CNBC-TV18 Prime is the first film and primary hero. History TV18 Sai Swad Dosa, CNBC Awaaz, Corner Seat and Mad About Markets provide featured film counterpoints: street reporting, performance, character and graphic energy. Sai Swad Dosa takes the wide Selected slot; Leadership remains in the full Film archive. The home page also carries Vodafone, the anniversary print campaign and the self-initiated Adidas work. Other films remain fully playable on the Film page and have individual project routes.

Every film uses the actual supplied aspect ratio. A 4:3 archive, a 5:4 Corner Seat export, a 1124 × 720 Forbes film and a 1920 × 1074 GST film are not forced into 16:9. Embedded letterboxing stays intact.

## Source quality and sizing

See SOURCE-COMPARISON.md for the per-film comparison. SD players have an intentional paper frame and desktop limits of 480 to 720px depending on source dimensions, with a 360px maximum on narrow screens. The 720p films have a maximum between 850 and 960px. Strong supplied HD films can use 1040 to 1100px. These are CSS-pixel presentation limits; user-initiated native fullscreen can enlarge a film at the viewer's choice. No video is cropped with cover or stretched by a fixed height.

The layout does not equate a 1080 filename with an original HD master. The selected 720p Bro Bytes files preserve the same composition at much lower bandwidth than their larger alternatives. Video files are stream-copied with fast-start metadata, not upscaled or sharpened. All are user-played, preload none, with poster frames and native audio, mobile and fullscreen controls.

## Digital work

Busy Message is one seven-screen walkthrough, not seven projects. The supplied standalone cover duplicates screen 02 and is not counted again. Screens retain their original 1024 × 768 canvas. The carousel supports native swiping, scroll snapping, keyboard arrows, Home/End, numbered progress and buttons. It never rotates automatically. An enlargement dialog provides a closer view while respecting image proportions. Original UI buttons inside screenshots are not presented as a live service.

ZooZoo is displayed as one archived interface. Emirates banners are grouped into one project and presented at 320 × 266, their supplied dimensions. Native play controls let visitors start and pause them. No reconstructed campaign animation is substituted.

## Print and speculative work

The anniversary campaign groups three executions. They use generous margins, changing scale and larger views. The Adidas concept groups Weightlessness, Momentum and Impulse in a staggered spread. Every relevant route labels it self-initiated and not commissioned by Adidas. It lives under AI because its imagery was AI-assisted. Experiments is hidden from the public categories until there is distinct additional work. The speculative status remains explicit.

## Landscapes

Three photos are prepared: the small rider in a huge mountain plain, a parked motorcycle at a mountain lake, and a rider on an empty road. Two are used as chapter pauses in V1. The third remains available in the identity data for future use. Other supplied photographs were inspected but omitted to keep the metaphor sparse. Portrait and travel photos never appear as client work in the index.

## Responsive layout

Desktop mixes unequal columns and offset placement. Mobile restages the portrait beneath the name, shifts smaller paper work to the edge, retains different film sizes, and rearranges the three speculative executions into one large image plus a pair. Navigation wraps rather than hiding categories behind an extra interaction. Index rows condense into readable project/metadata groups. Print and digital artwork itself is never cropped.

## Motion and accessibility

Only manual carousel scrolling and small control transitions are animated. Reduced-motion preferences remove smooth transitions. No autoplay, scroll hijacking, custom cursor, or persistent parallax. Pages include semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, labelled controls, native videos, and a native modal with Escape and focus restoration. Images include dimensions and alt text. The shared script pauses other videos when one plays.

## Scope and truthful metadata

Role credits supplied for V1.1 are now included for the Prime launch, History TV18 films and anniversary print campaign. Other unknown credits remain omitted. Unknown years are omitted. Forbes 2023 and the 2025 films have dates evidenced in filenames or artwork; the supplied new Adidas concept is 2026. Email and LinkedIn are not guessed. The portfolio is separate from acting, voice, agent and product sites. No em dash characters are present in the authored website copy.

## Verification and limits

Static routes, local links, assets, image dimensions, JavaScript syntax, media decoding and source byte counts are checked. The managed preview does not support this buildless static project, so browser-based visual review and live mobile/fullscreen interaction testing remain unverified. This is disclosed rather than represented as passed. Custom-domain DNS and TLS must be verified separately before the target hostname can be called live.

## V1.2 clarity and launch pass

Caption links had a blanket 14px rule that overrode the large headings on Print and AI. Heading links now inherit their heading size and line-height, with explicit charcoal colour and an understated underline in hover/focus states. No image overlays, gradients, cards or shadows were added. Small metadata contrast was strengthened within the same warm graphite palette. The title-link contrast against ivory is 12.67:1; hover/focus text is 5.46:1.

Tool credits are secondary definition-list metadata on four detail pages. The generated social preview uses the supplied portrait identity and existing palette, while all on-site artwork remains byte-identical. Navigation removes only Experiments. No autoplay, motion, player sizing, responsive composition, carousel logic or travel-image treatment changes were made.

The design-source audit found no generic feature-card system, pricing tiers, testimonials, emoji UI, icon library, gradient, glow, glass treatment, bento pattern, dot-grid surface or startup font dependency. Browser visual confirmation remains outstanding because this environment does not support a preview for the existing static stack.
